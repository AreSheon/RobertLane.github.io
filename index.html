Octopie - Y2 group project

This is my second year group project. I was the sole programmer in this project. My job involved creating the player controllers as well as designing and implementing the systems within the game that allow players to interact with the world and quests. These were designed to integrate with Unreal's Blueprint editor, as such I could offload the small changes between items to the others in the team to change in blueprints. I also worked closely with our animator to create the dynamic octopus, in which the limbs seamlessly change between being simulated and animated. Also sort many git issues.
