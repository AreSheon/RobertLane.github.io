Lost Frontiers

Overview

Lost Frontiers is a AA/AAA survival horror game that I am currently working on for my final year of my degree. The game is set in a pre-historic world where the player has time travelled back on a rescue mission. It will most likely have a steam punk aesthetic and various portions a big 'Stalker' enemy. For the module we are specifically creating a vertical slice of the game, in which I will explain the exact contents of later. My role is that of a backend and AI programmer of this project. The project will be developed in Unreal Engine 5 using C++ and Blueprints. I will be focusing on the Stalker AI for the most part as well as helping with backend systems the other 2 programmers cannot cover. I will also be working closely with the Animators for the Stalker enemy aswell as with the Sound designer with general technical help.
